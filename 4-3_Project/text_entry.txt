For this project, I backpedaled slightly from my original goal once I realized
it was beyond my capability to do in a single weekend.

This then almost moved to Morse code, but then I randomly thought of that
"Monsters Vs. Aliens" movie, and the two buttons scene, which also made me
think of some similar scenes for launching nukes, where there was a
two-key system. I wanted to riff on this, so I wrote some code (attached here)
that tracks the duration of a dual button press. If it greater than or equal to
10 seconds, the console prints "MISSILE ARMED". A little silly, but it was pretty
fun to put together!
