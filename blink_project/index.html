Blink Project: M-HALL Synth

For this demo, I wanted to incorporate a Piezo speaker into my project to make some music.

I was inspired by having to play recorder in elementary school, and wanted to make a simple 3-note synth replicating that simple, charming, and mildy annoying experience.
In order to get the Piezo working, I had to dig in a bit to the functioning of tone() in Arduino. I also had to rewire the Piezo to receive a digital signal instead of transmitting an analog response.

Thus the name M-HALL - short for Mary Had A Little Lamb, as that's one of the few playable songs.

The LEDs light up at the same time their respective button's note is played.

To get the respective pitches, I used this link to find the Piezo equivalencies to standard pitches.
