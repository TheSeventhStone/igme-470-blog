When looking at examples of interactive art, Random International's Rain Room really stood out to me. It's designed to encourage dialogue on the roles science and technology could play in stabilizing our environment through human ingenuity.

	3D tracking cameras control the output of 2500L of recycled water to simulate a 100m (~300 ft) square of rainfall that manages to never touch visitors. Despite hearing and seeing all of the "rain" around them, the precise control of the nozzles means the downpour moves around them. The experience itself lasts for about 15 minutes, with 6 visitors at a time.

There is only one permanent installation in Sharjah, but the piece itself has been hosted in museums such as MoMA and MoCA Busan.
